Engagement Process

Our Process

A clear, collaborative process keeps the work aligned from the first conversation through final delivery.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Define the actual question, problem, objective, and outcome the project needs to address.

  2. 02

    Planning

    Select the appropriate service structure and outline objectives, deliverables, timeline, and estimated investment.

  3. 03

    Development

    Complete the agreed-upon work while maintaining regular communication and progress updates.

  4. 04

    Review

    Present completed deliverables, gather feedback, and make final refinements where appropriate.

  5. 05

    Ongoing Support

    Clients may conclude the project or continue through an Operational Partnership for continued support and system improvement.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer standardized service packages?

No. Every engagement is customized to the client’s goals and project requirements.

Can multiple services be combined?

Yes. When the project genuinely requires it, service areas may work together within one defined engagement. Discovery is used to select only the research, organization, digital presence, or application work needed to solve the problem.

Do you work with individuals as well as organizations?

Yes. Miranda Insights works with individuals, entrepreneurs, businesses, schools, nonprofits, and community organizations.

How are projects priced?

Pricing varies depending on project scope, complexity, and estimated time requirements. A Discovery Session is the first step toward developing a customized proposal.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes. Clients may continue working with Miranda Insights through an Operational Partnership after project completion.
