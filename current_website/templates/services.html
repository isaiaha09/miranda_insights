Engagement Process

Our Process

A clear, collaborative process keeps the work aligned from the first conversation through final delivery.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Meet to discuss goals, challenges, project scope, and determine the most appropriate services.

  2. 02

    Planning

    Develop a project plan outlining objectives, deliverables, timeline, and estimated investment.

  3. 03

    Development

    Complete the agreed-upon work while maintaining regular communication and progress updates.

  4. 04

    Review

    Present completed deliverables, gather feedback, and make final refinements where appropriate.

  5. 05

    Ongoing Support

    Clients may conclude the project or continue through an Operational Partnership for continued support and system improvement.
